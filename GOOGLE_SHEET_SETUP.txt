================================================================================
RIDE SATHI GO - GOOGLE SHEET BACKUP & 7-DAY AUTO-CLEAN SETUP GUIDE
================================================================================
Project: ride-sathi-go
Admin Owner Email: support.ridesathi@gmail.com
Scheduled Cloud Function: deleteOldRidesAndBackup (Daily at 2:00 AM IST)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1: CREATE THE GOOGLE SHEET IN support.ridesathi@gmail.com
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Log into Google Drive / Google Sheets using: support.ridesathi@gmail.com
2. Create a new Google Sheet.
3. Rename the Spreadsheet to:
   "RIDE SATHI GO - Ride Database Backup"
4. Inside the Google Sheet, create 4 Tabs (Sheets at the bottom) named EXACTLY:
   - rides
   - completed_rides
   - ride_requests
   - accepted_rides

5. In Row 1 of EACH tab, add these 8 Column Headers (from Column A to H):
   A: date
   B: time
   C: city
   D: customer_mobile
   E: driver_mobile
   F: fare
   G: status
   H: ride_id

   IMPORTANT: All entries must be in ENGLISH ONLY (No Bengali text in Sheet).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2: GET YOUR GOOGLE SHEET ID
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Look at your Google Sheet browser address bar:
   https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1BxiMVs0XRA5nFMdKvBdBZjgmUUqptlbs74OgvE2upms/edit
                                         ^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^
   The long string between "/d/" and "/edit" is your SHEET ID.
2. Copy this SHEET ID.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3: SHARE SHEET WITH SERVICE ACCOUNT (EDITOR ACCESS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Download Service Account Private Key:
   - Go to Firebase Console: https://console.firebase.google.com/project/ride-sathi-go/settings/serviceaccounts/adminsdk
   - Click "Generate new private key".
   - Save the downloaded JSON file as:
     functions/service-account.json
   - Open this JSON and note the "client_email", for example:
     firebase-adminsdk-xxxxx@ride-sathi-go.iam.gserviceaccount.com

2. Share the Google Sheet:
   - In your Google Sheet, click the green "Share" button (top-right).
   - In the "Add people and groups" box, paste the service account email:
     firebase-adminsdk-xxxxx@ride-sathi-go.iam.gserviceaccount.com
   - Permission: Select "Editor".
   - (Uncheck "Notify people")
   - Click "Share".
   - Confirm support.ridesathi@gmail.com remains the Owner of the Sheet.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4: ENABLE GOOGLE SHEETS API IN GOOGLE CLOUD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open this link in your browser:
   https://console.cloud.google.com/apis/library/sheets.googleapis.com?project=ride-sathi-go
2. Click the blue "ENABLE" button.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5: CONFIGURE SHEET ID IN YOUR APP & FUNCTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Method A (From Admin Panel - Easiest):
1. Open admin.html -> Go to "📊 Google Sheet Reports" section.
2. Paste your Google Sheet ID in the input box and click "💾 Save Sheet ID".
3. Click "📊 Open Google Sheet Report" to verify it opens directly!

Method B (Via Firebase CLI):
Run in terminal:
firebase functions:config:set sheets.id="YOUR_GOOGLE_SHEET_ID"

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 6: DEPLOY CLOUD FUNCTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To deploy the scheduled 2:00 AM backup function:
1. Open PowerShell / Command Prompt in your project folder.
2. Run:
   firebase deploy --only functions

Once deployed, Google Cloud Scheduler will automatically trigger:
"deleteOldRidesAndBackup" every single night at 2:00 AM IST!
It will:
1. Find rides older than 7 days from rides, completed_rides, ride_requests, accepted_rides.
2. Copy them into the corresponding tabs of your Google Sheet.
3. Automatically delete completed rides (status=completed) from Firebase Realtime Database.
